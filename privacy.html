Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

LEVERAGE (including House Search, also called Deal Scout, and Job Tracker) is a private tool used by one person, its owner, on their own computer. It is not offered to anyone else.

What Google data it uses

  • Gmail, read only: it reads the owner's own saved-search listing alerts and job-related emails in the owner's own mailbox. It never sends, deletes or changes email.
  • Google Sheets, Drive and Apps Script: it updates the owner's own private spreadsheets and dashboards.

How the data is handled

  • Data stays on the owner's own computer and in the owner's own Google account.
  • It is not sold, shared with anyone else, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • Access can be removed at any time from the owner's Google Account security settings.

The use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
