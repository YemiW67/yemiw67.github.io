LEVERAGE

LEVERAGE is a private, personal-use set of tools that runs on one person's own computer. It includes House Search, which looks for homes to buy, and Job Tracker, which keeps track of job postings.

These tools are not offered to the public and have no other users. They connect to the owner's own Google account only, with the owner's permission, to read the owner's own email alerts and to keep the owner's own private spreadsheets and dashboards up to date.
